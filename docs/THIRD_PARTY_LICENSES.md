## Notes on specific licences

- **LGPL packages** (dompdf and its font/SVG libraries, smalot/pdfparser) are shipped unmodified in `vendor/` and can be replaced with another version through Composer, as the LGPL requires. Their source is available from their authors.
- **Dual-licensed packages** (nette/schema, nette/utils: BSD-3-Clause or GPL) are used under the BSD-3-Clause licence.
